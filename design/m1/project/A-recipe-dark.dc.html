Gochujang Butter Noodles

Glossy, spicy-sweet noodles from pantry staples. The weeknight default.

Prep 10 min · Cook 10 min

Servings Recipe makes 2
4

Ingredients

Scaled ×2
  • 12 ozdried wheat noodles
  • 4 tbspunsalted butter
  • 3 tbspgochujang
  • 2 tbspsoy sauce
  • 2 tsphoney
  • 4 clovesgarlic, grated
  • 4scallions, thinly sliced
  • —Toasted sesame seeds, to taste Not scaled — adjust by eye

Method

  1. 1Cook the noodles in salted boiling water until just tender. Reserve ½ cup of the cooking water, then drain.
  2. 2In the same pot over medium-low heat, melt the butter and cook the garlic for 30 seconds, until fragrant.
  3. 3Whisk in the gochujang, soy sauce and honey with 2 tbsp of the reserved water until glossy.
  4. 4Add the noodles and toss, loosening with more water a splash at a time.
  5. 5Top with the scallions and sesame seeds. Serve right away.
